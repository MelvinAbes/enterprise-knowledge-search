Application and Device Log Retention

Live search:
Application and device logs remain in the searchable hot tier for 90 days. Access to this tier
is limited to operations and security staff with a documented support or investigation need.

Archive:
Security-relevant audit events are copied to an encrypted archive for one year. Routine debug
events are not archived after their hot-tier period.

Deletion:
At the end of the applicable period, lifecycle jobs delete expired records and write aggregate
counts to the retention audit table. Legal holds suspend deletion only for the named data set.

Data minimisation:
Logs must not contain passwords, access tokens, full payment details, or document contents.
